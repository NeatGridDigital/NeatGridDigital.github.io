How Etsy fees work

Listing fee

About $0.20 (or the same in your currency) for each item sold. Each sale renews the listing, so the fee is charged again per unit.

Transaction fee: 6.5%

Charged on the item price plus the shipping you charge, after any discount.

Payment processing fee

A percentage plus a small fixed amount per order. For example 3% + $0.25 in the US, 4% + £0.20 in the UK, 4% + €0.30 in most EU countries.

Regulatory operating fee

An extra percentage in a few countries, such as the UK, France, Italy, Spain, Hungary and Canada.

Offsite Ads fee

15% of the order, only when the buyer came from an Etsy ad on another website. It drops to 12% once your shop has made $10,000 in a year, and it is capped per order.

VAT or GST on fees

Sellers in the UK, EU, Canada, Australia and New Zealand pay VAT or GST on top of Etsy's fees, unless they gave Etsy a VAT number. In Ireland it is always charged.

Do digital downloads pay the same fees?

Yes, the same listing, transaction and processing fees, just without postage.
