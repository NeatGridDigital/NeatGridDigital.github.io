Why your real hourly pay is lower

Fuel

Every mile or kilometre costs fuel. A car doing 30 mpg at $3.20 a gallon spends about 11 cents a mile.

Wear and repairs

Tyres, oil, brakes and services add up with distance. Many drivers put aside 8-15 cents a mile (or a few cents per km).

Fixed costs

Insurance, phone plan, parking and car washes are paid whether you drive or not. Split them over the hours you work.

Waiting time counts

Count every hour you are logged in and waiting, not just the hours with a passenger or an order.
