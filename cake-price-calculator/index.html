How to price a cake

1. Ingredients and packaging

Add up what this one order uses: flour, butter, eggs, chocolate, fondant, plus the box, board and toppers.

2. Pay yourself for your time

Count baking, decorating, shopping and cleaning. Pick an hourly pay you would accept from a job.

3. Add overheads

Your oven, mixer and electricity wear out and cost money. 10-15% on top of ingredients and time covers most kitchens.

4. Add profit

Profit is what the business keeps after paying you: for new tins, a slow month or growth.

Price per serving

Divide the price by the number of servings to compare with other bakers and to quote cupcakes or slices.
