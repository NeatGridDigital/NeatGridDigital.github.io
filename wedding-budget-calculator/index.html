How to split a wedding budget

Start with the guest list

Venue and food are the biggest costs and grow with every guest. Ten fewer guests often saves more than any other cut.

Keep a buffer

Small extras add up: tips, alterations, postage, last-minute decor. Keep 5-10% aside.

Decide your top three

Pick the three things that matter most to you both, spend more there, and save on the rest.
