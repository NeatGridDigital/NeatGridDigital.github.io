Making Tax Digital in short

What changes?

Instead of one tax return a year, you keep digital records and send a short summary to HMRC every quarter through software, then a final return after the year.

Who has to join?

Sole traders and landlords whose qualifying income is over the limit: over £50,000 from April 2026, over £30,000 from April 2027, over £20,000 from April 2028. HMRC looks at the tax return from two years before.

Quarterly deadlines

Updates are due 7 August, 7 November, 7 February and 7 May. The final return and payment are due 31 January after the tax year.

Can I use a spreadsheet?

Yes, you can keep your records in a spreadsheet and send them with bridging software.
