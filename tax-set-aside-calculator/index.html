Saving for tax when you're self-employed

Why save from every payment?

No employer takes tax out for you. Moving a fixed share of each payment to a separate account means the bill is never a surprise.

Save from profit, not income

Tax is usually charged on profit: income minus allowable business expenses. Keep receipts so your expenses count.

What rate should I use?

Many freelancers start at 25-30% of profit and adjust after their first tax bill. Higher earners often need more.

Quarterly payments

In some countries (for example US estimated tax, UK payments on account) you pay during the year. A quarterly figure helps you plan for those.
