Reseller fees in short

Vinted

Sellers pay no selling fee. The buyer pays a buyer protection fee. Optional bumps and spotlights cost extra.

eBay

Private sellers in the UK pay no final value fee in most categories; EU countries vary. In the US, most categories cost about 13.6% of the total including shipping, plus $0.40 per order ($0.30 for orders of $10 or less).

Depop

No selling fee in the UK and US, but a payment processing fee: 2.9% + £0.30 in the UK, 3.3% + $0.45 in the US, on the price plus shipping.

Poshmark and Mercari

Poshmark takes a flat $2.95 on sales under $15 and 20% on sales of $15 or more. Mercari takes 10% of the item price plus shipping.

Do I have to report my sales?

In the EU and UK, platforms report sellers with 30 or more sales or €2,000 (about £1,700) or more in a year to the tax office. In the US, platforms send a 1099-K above $20,000 and 200 sales. Selling your own used things at a loss is usually not taxable, but buying to resell is a business. Not tax advice.
